File format

Only multiple-choice (single correct answer) questions are supported. In the Google Doc, write each question as a block of lines with a blank line between questions — any order of lines within a block is fine. {% if sections %}Sections created for this test: {% for s in sections %}{{ s.name }}{% if not forloop.last %}, {% endif %}{% endfor %}.{% endif %}

LineRequiredWhat to put
Q1. … or [Q] …YesThe question text. A leading Q1., 1) or [Q] is optional and stripped automatically.
A) … / (a) … … D) … / (d) …At least 2 optionsThe answer choices, one per line, letter followed by ), ., :, or wrapped in ( ). Leave the text after the letter blank if the option is only shown in a figure/image.
Answer: … or [ANS] …No, but recommendedThe correct option's letter (e.g. Answer: B or [ANS] b) or its full text
Solution: … or [SOL] …NoOptional explanation shown to students under "View Solutions" after they submit. Can sit on its own line, even after a blank line.
Marks: …No — defaults to 1Whole number of marks for the question
Section: …No — defaults to unassignedMust exactly match an existing section name for this test
[HI] … / [KN] …NoHindi/Kannada translation of the line directly above it — put it right after the Q1. … question line to translate the question, or right after an A) …/B) …/etc. option line to translate that option. Not supported for Answer/Solution/Marks/Section lines.

To add Hindi or Kannada text, insert a [HI] or [KN] line immediately below the English line it translates — one translation line per language, right after the question or the option it belongs to. Missing translations are fine; students just see the English version for that language. This works the same on Google Docs, .docx and .txt uploads.

Example question block

A blank line separates this question from the next one in the doc.

Q1. What is the capital of India? A) Mumbai B) New Delhi C) Kolkata D) Chennai Answer: B Solution: New Delhi has been the capital of India since 1911. Marks: 1
With Hindi & Kannada translations

[HI]/[KN] lines go right after the line they translate — question or option.

Q1. What is the capital of India? [HI] भारत की राजधानी क्या है? [KN] ಭಾರತದ ರಾಜಧಾನಿ ಯಾವುದು? A) Mumbai [HI] मुंबई [KN] ಮುಂಬೈ B) New Delhi [HI] नई दिल्ली [KN] ಹೊಸ ದೆಹಲಿ C) Kolkata D) Chennai Answer: B
[Q] / [ANS] / [SOL] style also works

Pasted straight from a PDF or textbook — options can be blank when they're only shown in a figure.

[Q] Identify the correct circuits from the figures provided. (a) (b) (c) (d) [ANS] b [SOL] In figure B, the voltmeter, ammeter and the resistance are connected correctly.
